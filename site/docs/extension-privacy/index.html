Extension privacy and permissions

No site access at install, nothing read before you click Send, and nothing sent anywhere except SessionForge on 127.0.0.1.

What it reads, and when#

  • Send to SessionForge: the cookies of the site you send (what Chrome sends to that page, plus the site's domain cookies), that page's localStorage, and the page URL, title and favicon URL.
  • Send to <session>: only the cookies of the domains shown on that button.
  • Only when you click a Send button. Nothing is read in the background.

Where it goes#

Only to SessionForge on your computer: POST http://127.0.0.1:34567/import. Opening the popup also asks GET http://127.0.0.1:34567/pending which sessions wait for a login. After you agreed, at most once a day, it asks GitHub for the latest SessionForge version number; that request carries none of your data. No analytics, telemetry or remote code.

Permissions#

PermissionWhy
activeTabThe current tab's address and title in the popup
cookiesRead the site's cookies when you click Send
scriptingRead the page's localStorage when you click Send
storageYour answer to the notice and the last version check
http://127.0.0.1:34567/*Talk to SessionForge's import server
*://*/* (optional)Asked per site, only when you send from it

To stop it, remove site access in chrome://extensions → SessionForge Import → Details → Site access, or uninstall it. Full statement: store/PRIVACY.md.
