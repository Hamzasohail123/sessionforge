Split view

Switch between single, two columns, two rows and a 2x2 grid with the layout buttons at the right of the session bar.

Panes#

Each pane shows one tab from any session, under a header with the session and page name. Click a pane (or press Cmd/Ctrl+1 to 4) to focus it. The tab bar, address bar and session bar then act on that pane. Clicking a session fills the focused pane with that session's last tab.

Layout and pane contents are restored on the next start.

New tabs and popups#

Context-menu "open in new tab" items, Cmd/Ctrl+U and window.open() popups open in the pane they came from, in the same session. DevTools open in a separate window.

One Google account per Chrome profile. When several Google accounts are signed in to one Chrome profile, every session sent to SessionForge from that profile carries all of them, and signing out in one can sign out the others.
