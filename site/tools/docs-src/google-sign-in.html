SessionForge does not disguise itself to get around this: its user agent and fingerprint stay as they are. Instead, when a pane reaches a Google sign-in page, a banner in that pane offers Sign in with Chrome.

The full flow, requirements and limits are on Sign in with Chrome.

Unverified: Chrome can bind Google sessions to the device (Device Bound Session Credentials). If your Google session is bound this way, the copy in SessionForge may sign out after a while; sign in with Chrome again if it does.
