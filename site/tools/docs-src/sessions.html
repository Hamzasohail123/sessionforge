Create

Click New session, enter a name, pick a colour and an environment: Local, Staging, Production or Custom. A session's environment can't be changed after it is created.

Rename and recolour

Double-click a session in the session bar (or focus it and press F2), type, then Enter saves and Escape cancels. Clicking elsewhere also saves; an empty name keeps the old one. While renaming, click the dot to pick a colour.

Close and reopen

The × next to a session closes it after a confirmation. Its tabs close, but its logins stay on disk, and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+T reopens it with its tabs. The same shortcut reopens closed tabs, newest first.

SessionForge keeps your last 10 closed sessions and your last 25 closed tabs. Closing an 11th session deletes the oldest closed session for good, including its logins. Closing tabs never deletes a closed session.

Delete permanently

Delete permanently in the close confirmation removes the session's cookies and storage. It can't be reopened.

Where data lives

All session data stays on your computer: ~/.config/sessionforge/ on Linux, ~/Library/Application Support/sessionforge/ on macOS and %APPDATA%\sessionforge\ on Windows.
