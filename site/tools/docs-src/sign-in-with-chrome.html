1. In the pane's banner, click Sign in with Chrome. Your browser opens the right Google sign-in page (or the site's login page for "Sign in with Google" on another site).
  2. Sign in there as usual.
  3. Click the SessionForge Import extension and choose Send to <session> (SessionForge asked for this).

The login goes into the session that asked for it (no new session), and the waiting pane continues where it left off. From the keyboard: press Cmd/Ctrl+L, then Tab until the banner's button is focused, and Enter.

Requirements and limits

  • SessionForge Import 1.2.0 or later in the browser that opens. SessionForge tries Chrome, then Chromium, Edge and Brave, else your default browser.
  • SessionForge's import server on its default port 34567 (not changed with SESSIONFORGE_IMPORT_PORT).
  • A request is valid for 10 minutes and works once.
  • Only cookies are sent for a request. Sites that keep their login in localStorage or IndexedDB stay signed out; use Send to new session for those.
  • For your own app the site must use https, except on localhost.
